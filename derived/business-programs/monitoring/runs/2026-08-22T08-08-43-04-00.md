- Status: ACTIVE / newly surfaced in IS&S monitoring index; not established as newly opened today.
- Benefit: free business courses, tailored training and one-on-one consulting focused on government-contracting readiness; includes finding/bidding/completing contracts, business development, strategic planning, accounting, marketing, financial analysis, compliance, cybersecurity and technology.
